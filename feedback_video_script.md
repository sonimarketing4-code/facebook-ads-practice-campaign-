# Project Feedback Video Script

Hello, my name is Durgesh Sharma.

For my Unified Mentor project, I worked on a Facebook Ads Practice Campaign for a fictional café called UrbanBite Café.

The main objective of the campaign was to create awareness and drive traffic to a fictional website or landing page.

I worked on the campaign proposal, target audience, budget allocation, ad formats and ad copy. I also prepared concepts for image, carousel and short-video advertisements.

For reporting, I used simulated campaign data to understand metrics such as impressions, reach, clicks, CTR, CPC and CPM. I have clearly marked these results as sample figures because this was a practice project and no real advertising spend was used.

The main things I learned were how to structure an advertising campaign, define an audience, create different ad formats and analyze campaign performance.

One challenge was designing a realistic campaign without using a real business. This helped me understand how marketers can plan and test campaigns before launching them.

Overall, this project gave me practical exposure to the basic workflow of Facebook Ads campaign planning and reporting.

Thank you.
