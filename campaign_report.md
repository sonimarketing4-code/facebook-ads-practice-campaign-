# Facebook Ads Practice Campaign – UrbanBite Café

## 1. Executive Summary
This project demonstrates the planning, creation, setup and analysis of a fictional Facebook Ads campaign for **UrbanBite Café**, a café concept. The campaign is designed to generate website traffic and increase awareness among young urban customers.

The campaign uses three creative formats: a single-image ad, a carousel ad and a short video ad. A simulated budget of **₹10,000** is allocated for seven days.

> **Academic simulation:** No real advertising campaign was run. All performance figures in the analytics section are mock/sample figures for demonstrating the reporting process.

## 2. Business Description
**UrbanBite Café** is a fictional café offering affordable coffee, beverages, snacks and quick meals for students and working professionals.

**Tagline:** Fresh. Fast. Made for Your Day.

### Value proposition
- Affordable café meals and beverages
- Quick service
- Comfortable environment
- Simple online ordering/visit information

## 3. Campaign Proposal

### Campaign objective
Drive website visits and build awareness for a fictional café offering affordable meals, snacks and beverages.

### Campaign goals
1. Increase awareness of the café concept.
2. Generate visits to the campaign landing page.
3. Test different creative formats.
4. Identify which audience and creative combinations produce better engagement.

### Campaign duration
7 days.

### Practice budget
₹10,000 total.

### Budget allocation
| Ad set | Daily budget | 7-day budget |
|---|---:|---:|
| Students & young adults | ₹700 | ₹4,900 |
| Young professionals | ₹500 | ₹3,500 |
| Retargeting/engaged users | ₹230 | ₹1,610 |
| Testing reserve | — | -₹10 |
| **Total** | | **₹10,000** |

For a real implementation, the budget would be adjusted based on platform delivery and campaign performance.

## 8. Creative Strategy
The campaign uses a clean, friendly visual style with food photography, simple headlines and a clear call-to-action.

Creative testing compares:
- Food-focused image
- Product/coffee-focused image
- Café ambience
- Short video

The purpose is to identify which creative generates stronger click-through and engagement.

## 9. Mock Analytics & Reporting
The following are **simulated results**, created only to demonstrate how campaign reporting would be presented.

| Metric | Sample result |
|---|---:|
| Spend | ₹10,000 |
| Impressions | 34,800 |
| Reach | 28,500 |
| Link clicks | 1,450 |
| CTR | 4.17% |
| CPC | ₹6.90 |
| CPM | ₹287.36 |
| Landing-page views | 1,180 |

### Interpretation
- A 4.17% CTR indicates that the sample creative generated a meaningful level of click interest in this hypothetical scenario.
- The sample CPC is calculated as spend divided by link clicks.
- Landing-page views are lower than link clicks because not every click necessarily results in a completed page load.
- These figures are not actual Meta/Facebook results.

## 10. Optimization Plan
If this were a live campaign:
1. Compare CTR and CPC across creatives.
2. Shift more budget toward creatives producing efficient traffic.
3. Refresh ads if frequency becomes high.
4. Test different headlines and opening lines.
5. Test audience segments separately.
6. Review landing-page experience and loading speed.
7. Use retargeting for people who engaged but did not visit/convert.

## 11. Challenges
- Designing a realistic campaign without a real business.
- Creating multiple creative formats.
- Choosing a suitable audience.
- Understanding campaign metrics and their relationships.
- Distinguishing clicks, landing-page views, reach and impressions.
